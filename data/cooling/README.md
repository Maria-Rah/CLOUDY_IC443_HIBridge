# Cooling function

`m-00.cie`: cooling function of solar-metallicity gas in collisional ionization equilibrium,
log T = 4.0-8.5, from Sutherland, R. S., & Dopita, M. A. 1993, ApJS, 88, 253 (doi:10.1086/191823),
as distributed by the authors (www.mso.anu.edu.au/~ralph/data/cool/). SHA-1 of the file:
34b041f8241bebada7bbc5aa3791d7b80f346ca1.

Columns used by `scripts/revision_numbers.py`: log T, n_e, n_H, n_t (per hydrogen), and
log(Lambda_net), the net cooling rate per unit volume for n_H = 1 cm^-3 (erg cm^3 s^-1).
